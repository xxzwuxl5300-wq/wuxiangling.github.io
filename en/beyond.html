01
Resilience
Former Dance-Troupe Captain · Performer

Classical Chinese Dance

Dance is where I first learned what resilience feels like. While preparing for university arts-specialty examinations in my final year of high school, I trained until midnight after completing a full day of study—then returned to class through exhaustion and soreness, travelling to different universities on weekends for auditions. Later, as a dance-troupe captain, I led the campus team to its first breakthrough in the three-campus dance competition. The discipline to keep moving when progress is painful still supports how I approach research, work and uncertainty.

“Every day without dance is a day I have not fully honored life.”

Competitive Drive
Institute for Environment and Ecology Badminton Team

Badminton

Badminton turns competitive drive into action. Every rally tests strategy, anticipation, reaction speed, physical conditioning and the ability to adjust under pressure. As a member of the badminton team of Tsinghua University's Institute for Environment and Ecology, I enjoy the discipline of training and the intensity of competing for a shared result. That same energy leads me to keep expanding my physical boundaries through table tennis, skiing, surfing and skating.

03
Curiosity & Exploration
Certified Diver · Underwater Explorer

Scuba Diving

Diving represents my instinct to explore what is unfamiliar. Beneath the surface, the world follows a different rhythm: attention becomes sharper, movement becomes more deliberate and curiosity opens into discovery. Earning a diving certification was not an endpoint, but a passport to keep learning about marine life, underwater environments and the limits of my own perception.

04
Insight & Empathy
Psychology-Center Assistant · Association Vice President

Psychology & Community

Psychology trains me to look beneath visible behavior and ask what truly drives a person, a group or a decision. My reading ranges from group and social psychology to Adler, Freud, Carnegie and nonviolent communication. Practice as a psychology-center assistant and Vice President of the Tsinghua SIGS Student Psychological Association taught me to listen without rushing to judgment, receive emotion before responding, communicate with greater care and search for the underlying logic behind what people say and do.
