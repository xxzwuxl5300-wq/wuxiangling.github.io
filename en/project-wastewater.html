Problem

Conventional nitrogen removal depends on costly external carbon sources and carries a high carbon footprint, while food-waste hydrolysate remains underutilized.

My role & method

Developed a process coupling carbon-source recovery from food waste with short-cut denitrification and anammox. Integrated solid-waste valorization, process optimization, patent development and demonstration delivery.

Key result

Replaced conventional chemicals with recovered solid-waste resources, reducing operating costs by 77%. Coordinated end-to-end delivery and independently completed a 100,000-word final report and defense.

Industrial value

A deployable low-carbon water-treatment pathway that connects waste-resource recovery with measurable plant economics.
