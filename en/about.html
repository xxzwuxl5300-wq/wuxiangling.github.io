About · Xiangling Wu

Built for the spaces between disciplines.

A research-led path from environmental engineering to industrial delivery and investment research.

Researcher · Builder · Analyst

I work at the intersection of environmental science, technology commercialization and investment research - translating complex technical systems into measurable industrial value and informed capital decisions.

My path began with environmental engineering research: microbial electrochemistry, greenhouse-gas mitigation and low-carbon wastewater treatment. It expanded into demonstration engineering, patents and cross-organization delivery, then into deep-tech and fundamental investment research. This combination lets me examine a technology from mechanism to deployment, and from industry structure to investment conviction.
