Problem

Low agricultural-waste utilization, high fresh-food losses and pollution from conventional packaging demand an integrated material solution.

My role & method

Combined functional extraction from waste streams with interfacial self-assembly to develop a protein-based bionanocomposite coating. Iteratively optimized material preparation and protection performance.

Key result

Extended fruit and vegetable shelf life by 2-5x and reduced application-stage carbon emissions by 90%.

Industrial value

An integrated path from agricultural-waste valorization to low-carbon preservation and packaging, with competition and commercialization potential.
